About Me

I recently graduated with a B.Sc. in Computer Science and Engineering from Daffodil International University, Dhaka. During my undergraduate studies, I worked as a researcher in the DIU NLP & ML Lab, where I contributed to multiple peer-reviewed publications spanning Natural Language Processing, Computer Vision, transfer learning, and explainable AI — including work on insect diversity classification, skin disease classification, fraudulent transaction detection, and medical image segmentation.

My research interests center on Natural Language Processing, Large Language Models, Computer Vision, and multimodal learning. I have been admitted to Boise State University as a Graduate Teaching Assistant, where I will begin my doctoral studies in Computer Science and continue pursuing research in these areas.
